I'm Omkar Sanjeev, an Electronics and Communication Engineering student with a parallel life in visual media. I lead CineLab, the photography and videography wing under Spectrum, where I design curriculum, mentor members, and build a culture around seeing the world thoughtfully.

As Design Head, I translate ideas into visuals — event posters, social media creatives, and brand communications that speak before anyone reads a word.

Every frame I shoot and every canvas I design starts with one question: what should someone feel when they look at this? The answer drives everything else.
